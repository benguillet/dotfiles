# Flight Preferences

Ben's personal flight preferences. Apply these when ranking or filtering flight options; they are preferences, not hard rules — show a violating option only when it is clearly better on price or availability, and flag the violation.

## Preferences

| Preference | Detail |
|---|---|
| Direct flights | Prefer nonstop over connections |
| Starlink Wi-Fi | Prefer aircraft equipped with Starlink when possible. Rolling out on Air France (free with Flying Blue account), United, Hawaiian, Qatar, and others — check per-aircraft equipment, rollouts are gradual |
| No early departures | Avoid departures before 9:00am local time |
| No short red-eyes | No red-eye (overnight) flights when scheduled duration is under 9 hours; red-eyes acceptable on 9hr+ long-haul |

## Flying between San Francisco and Nantes (SFO ↔ NTE)

- **Flying Blue sweet spot:** Air France sometimes prices SFO↔NTE awards at the same Flying Blue points cost as SFO↔CDG alone (the NTE leg is effectively free). Always check both SFO↔NTE and SFO↔CDG award pricing.
- **Train alternative:** Also consider flying SFO↔CDG and taking the TGV from the CDG airport train station (Aéroport CDG 2 TGV) direct to Nantes — compare total cost and time against the connecting flight.
- **Other airlines welcome:** Not Air France–only; include other carriers, especially when the price is much lower.

## How to apply

- Rank compliant itineraries first; annotate any recommended option that violates a preference (e.g. "departs 7:40am — before your 9am cutoff").
- For the red-eye rule: an overnight long-haul (9hr+) is fine and often preferable; a 5hr overnight transcon is not.
- When sources expose Wi-Fi/equipment data, note which options have Starlink.
